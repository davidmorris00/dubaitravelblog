How Do Play DXB Dubai Ticket Options Work?

Play DXB uses a credit-based system. Visitors purchase or load credits onto a digital card and use them to access participating games and rides. Each activity requires a specific number of credits, so the total number of experiences depends on the selected ticket option and how the balance is used.

Standard Pay and Play Credits

  • Advertised price range in the supplied article: AED 200–300.
  • Credits may be used across eligible VR rides and games.

Combo and Bonus Credit Options

  • Advertised price range in the supplied article: AED 300–500.
